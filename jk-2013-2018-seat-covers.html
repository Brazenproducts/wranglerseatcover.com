Frequently Asked Questions

Do 2013-2018 JK and JKU use the same seat covers?

The front seat covers are identical. Rear covers differ — 2-door JK has a smaller rear bench than the 4-door JKU. Always specify door count when ordering.

Are Bartact covers worth the price over Amazon options?

Bartact uses 600D Polyester with PU waterproof backing, laminated foam and scrim, and mil-spec MOLLE — built in the USA. Amazon options are functional but use lighter materials without MOLLE. If you wheel hard, Bartact is worth it.

Will seat covers void my Jeep warranty?

No — aftermarket seat covers do not void your warranty. Just ensure covers are SAB (side airbag) compliant, which all picks on this page are.
