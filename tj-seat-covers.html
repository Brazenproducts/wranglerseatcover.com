Frequently Asked Questions

Do TJ and LJ (Unlimited) use the same seat covers?

Yes — front covers are identical. The LJ has the same front seat dimensions as the TJ. Both use the same Bartact and Amazon covers.

Did the TJ have side airbags?

Most TJ trims did NOT have SAB side airbags — check your specific vehicle. If no SAB, any seat cover fits. If your TJ has SABs, use only SAB-compliant covers.
