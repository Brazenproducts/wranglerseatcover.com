Frequently Asked Questions

Do JL and JLU use the same seat covers?

Front covers are identical. Rear bench differs — JL 2-door has a smaller rear than JLU 4-door. Specify door count when ordering.

Why won't Bartact covers fit the Mojave or 392 Edition?

These editions have heated/ventilated seats with additional HVAC cutouts. Standard JL covers block those vents. Bartact makes edition-specific covers — check their site for Mojave/392 fitment.
